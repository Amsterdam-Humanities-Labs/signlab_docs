# Logging in

One account gives you access to all SignCollect interfaces. You log in once;
every page on signcollect.nl then knows who you are.

## Get an account

There is no sign-up page. Ask an administrator to create an account for you.
They set your user name, password, language (Dutch or English) and role.

## Log in

1. Go to <https://signcollect.nl/login.html>.
2. Enter your user name in **Gebruikersnaam** and your password in
   **Wachtwoord**.
3. Click **Inloggen**.

You land on the start page, **Kies een interface**. It has two tiles:
**signCollect interface (nieuw)** (gloss management) and **Zinnen interface**.

If you opened a SignCollect page while logged out, that page sends you to the
login page first. After you log in, you go straight back to the page you
wanted.

## Error messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Ongeldige gebruikersnaam of wachtwoord. | The user name or password is wrong. | Check the spelling and caps lock, then try again. |
| Uw account is geblokkeerd. Neem contact op met een beheerder. | Your account is blocked. | Ask an administrator to unblock it. See below. |
| Er is een fout opgetreden. Probeer het opnieuw. | The server did not answer. | Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps happening, see [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/index.md). |

## How long you stay logged in

Your session lasts one year. You stay logged in on that browser until you log
out, clear your cookies, or the year is over.

!!! warning "Accounts are blocked after 60 days"
    If you have not used SignCollect for more than 60 days, your account is
    blocked the next time you log in with your password. You then see
    *Uw account is geblokkeerd*. Ask an administrator to unblock it.

A session belongs to one browser. On a second computer, or in a private
window, you log in again.

## Log out

Log out on a shared computer, such as the studio or lab machines.

1. Click the log-out icon at the top right of the gloss management page. You
   can also open the menu and choose **Uitloggen**, or go to
   <https://signcollect.nl/logout.html>.
2. On the **Uitloggen** page, click **Uitloggen** again to confirm, or
   **Annuleren** to stay logged in.

You return to the login page.

## Forgotten your password?

SignCollect has no "forgot password" link. Ask an administrator to set a new
password for you. They do this on the **Gebruikers Beheren** page.
